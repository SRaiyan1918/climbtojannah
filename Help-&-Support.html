Frequently Asked Questions

How does the points system work?
Each good deed earns you positive points based on its spiritual significance. Bad deeds have negative points. Your total points represent your progress on the path to Jannah. Completing predefined deeds like prayers gives higher points, while custom deeds have balanced points.
Can I add my own custom deeds?
Yes! You can add both good and bad deeds. The app automatically assigns appropriate points based on the deed type. Good deeds typically get 30-40 points, while bad deeds to avoid have -60 to -70 points.
How is my progress tracked?
We track your daily progress and lifetime achievements. The app records how many times you complete each deed and shows your most frequent deeds in the progress tracking section. Your data is stored locally on your device.
What happens if I miss a day?
Don't worry! The app automatically resets daily counts each new day while preserving your lifetime progress. Every day is a fresh start to improve and get closer to Jannah.
Is my data secure and private?
Absolutely! All your data is stored locally on your device. We don't collect or store any personal information on our servers. Your spiritual journey remains completely private between you and Allah.
